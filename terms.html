Section 13

How the AI works, and what it cannot do

The Concierge uses artificial intelligence, including large language models supplied by third parties, to understand what a person is asking and to generate a reply. This is powerful, and it is not perfect. You should read this section as a description of what you are buying.

  • Outputs can be wrong. The Concierge can misunderstand, give an incomplete answer, or state something inaccurate, even when it is configured correctly. We build guardrails, test, and refine, but we do not warrant that any particular Output will be accurate, complete, or suitable.
  • Outputs are not advice. Nothing the Concierge says is professional, medical, legal, financial, or safety advice, and it must not be presented to your customers as such.
  • Not for emergencies. Neither you nor an End user may rely on the Concierge in an emergency or in any situation where a delay or an error could cause harm. End users should contact emergency services directly.
  • Bookings and quotes. Where the Concierge books an appointment, gives a price, quotes a callout, or makes any commitment, it does so from the Client Content and rules you approved. Those Outputs are made on your behalf and you are responsible for them as between you and your customer. We recommend configuring quotes as estimates subject to your confirmation, and we will help you set that up.
  • Human oversight is required. Decisions that matter, such as accepting a customer, fulfilling an order, or confirming an appointment, must involve a person at your business.
  • Disclosure. We tell people plainly that they are speaking to an automated assistant, and offer a route to a human. This is not optional.
  • Model dependencies. The Services depend on AI providers whose availability, pricing, and policies can change. We may substitute providers or models to keep the Services running or to improve them.
  • No training on your conversations. We do not use End user conversation content to train our own or third parties' AI models. See our Privacy Policy.
In short: the Concierge is an excellent front desk, and it is not a guarantee. Configure it carefully, review what it says, and keep a person in the loop for anything consequential.
